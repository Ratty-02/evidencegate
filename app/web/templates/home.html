The gate

Five checks. Nothing skips them.

  1. 1

    Dispute open

    Evidence is only sent while PayPal still accepts it.

  2. 2

    File rules

    Accepted types and size limits, checked before upload.

  3. 3

    Claims grounded

    Every claim cites a real record, with the value matching exactly.

  4. 4

    Sentences covered

    Any sentence no claim supports blocks submission.

  5. 5

    You approve

    Nothing is sent until a person slides to approve.

Why it matters

Trust is the product.

01

The model drafts. The gate decides.

Language models are fluent, not reliable. EvidenceGate treats every sentence as a claim to verify, and blocks anything it cannot trace to a record.

02

Honest by design

Blocked drafts can't be approved, even by a click. Every approval stores a request ID for audit.

03

Measured, not claimed

Labeled cases with injected errors show how often the gate catches them.

04

Built on PayPal's own rules

Dispute status, the provide-evidence link, file types, and size limits all come from PayPal's Disputes API.
